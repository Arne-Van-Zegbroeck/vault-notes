



### Control
You gain control over the storm inside you granting you perfect control over the water surrounding you. You permanently have the calm spells prepared and permanently loose access to the storm spells, even when innate sorcery is active. 
You always have [[Tidal Surge]], 
Additionally, you can feel the water flowing through your surroundings, not only in the land but also through other living things. You gain the following ability.

**Blood Magic.** As a magic action, you can manipulate the water flowing through a plant or creature to either reinvigorate them, dry them out or control.
- **Reinvigorate.** You instill life into the greater granting the following effects to the target.
	- remove all Exhaustion
	- remove the Charmed, Frightened, Petrified, Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, Poisoned and Stunned condition
	- remove all reduction's to all the target's ability scores
	- remove all reduction's to the target's Hit Points
	- Restore 60 Hit Points to the Target
- **Dry.** You drain the water from a living creature, the target must make a constitution saving throw. If the target fails, they take 6d12 necrotic damage, gain the blinded condition, their movement is halved for an hour and they gain 4 levels of Exhaustion.  
- **Control.** Requires concentration. The target must make a constitution saving throw. On a fail you take control over the liquid inside the target's body. On your turn , you can use your bonus action to command the target during their turn to use the attack action, move action or other physical actions. You can make the target take a Reaction but must take your own Reaction to do so. Additionally, you can using your Reaction make the target fail Strength, Dexterity and Constitution saving throws. The target can redo the constitution saving throw at the end of their turn.
