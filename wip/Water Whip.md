Source: Arne
*Level 1 Conjuration (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You blast a stream of water in front of you. Each creature in a 30-foot-long, 5-foot wide Line originating from you must make Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d8 Bludgeoning damage and is prone. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only. 

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage and length of the line increase by 1d8 and 30 feet respectively for every spell slot above 1.
