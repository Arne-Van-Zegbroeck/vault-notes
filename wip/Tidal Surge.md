Source: Arne
*Level 2 Conjuration (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M ()
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a surging wave that drags creatures from one point to another. Choose a point you can see within range. Each creature in a 5-foot radius centred on that point must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature is swept up by a 15 foot wide wave.

You then choose a second point you can see within range that has a clear, unobstructed line from the first point. Each creature swept up by the wave is dragged in a straight line to the second point. If the wave passes through other creatures, those creatures must also make a Dexterity saving throw or be dragged as well.

All creatures dragged by the wave are placed in unoccupied spaces of your choice within 5 feet of the second point and fall prone. Creatures that succeed on their saving throw are not affected.


**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the initial radius and final placement radius each increase by 5 feet, the wave’s width increases by 10 feet and the range increases by 30 feet for each slot level above 2nd. Instead of applying one of these increases, you may choose to forgo it. If you do, you may select which creatures are affected by the wave rather than affecting all creatures in the area.
