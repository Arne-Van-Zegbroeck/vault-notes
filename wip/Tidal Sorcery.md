You release the storm raging in you while your innate sorcery is active. While your innate sorcery is active, you gain access to the spells in the Storm table and loose access to the spells in the Calm table.

## Level 3: Form of Water
You become attuned to the water around you. You gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed and can breathe underwater. 

Additionally, as a Magic action you can create a body of water filling a cubic area up to 5 feet x your sorcerer level on a side. This water lasts for 1 minute and requires concentration (as if concentrating on a spell). The water disappears early if you dismiss it (no action required) or if your concentration ends.

While concentrating on this created water, you can maintain and control it without expending your concentration. Spells and features that manipulate water (such as _control water_) can target and affect this created water, but if your concentration on this feature ends, you immediately lose control of all effects manipulating it.

## Level 6: Increased Control
Water becomes very easy for you to manipulate. When casting spells the prepared spells from this subclass, you can add one Metamagic for free. You can do this a number of times equal to your charisma modifier (minimum 1). 

## Level 14: 
You can create a magical link between either two body's of water of at least 5 cubic feet on the same plane of existence together or link 2 5 cubic feet area's of the same body of water together. You must have seen or touched the destination body at least once before. For the next minute, any creature can step in the water and exit from the destination water body by using 5 feet of movement. You can do this once per long rest or use 5 sorcery points to refresh this ability. 

## Level 18: Avatar of Water
While your innate sorcery is active, you submit to the storm inside you. Your body becomes more liquid and translucent granting you the following properties while Innate Sorcery is active:
- You gain immunity to fire damage.
- You can move through spaces as narrow as one inch wide counting it as Difficult terrain, but you are not allowed to end your turn in a narrow space.
